# Expositiones Mathematicae: submission notes

Prepared on 9 October 2026.

## Article category and formatting basis

The suggested category is Survey Article. The journal's official scope describes these as well-organized, critical and in-depth expositions of contemporary mathematical research, accessible to research students and mathematicians outside the immediate specialty. Survey articles have no stated page limit on that scope page.

The manuscript uses Elsevier's official elsarticle author class and the elsarticle-num numerical bibliography style. The compact PDF and review PDF contain the same article. The compact version is an author-generated journal-style reading layout, not a publisher-issued version of record.

The journal-specific ScienceDirect Guide for Authors returned HTTP 403 during this preparation. Accordingly, journal-specific requirements that could not be read are not represented as verified. Before uploading, check the live Guide for Authors for any required submission items, current declarations, reference details and manuscript-file preferences. The publisher's general LaTeX guidance and the journal's publicly accessible scope were available and used.

## Submission files

Fill in the actual author names, affiliations, corresponding-author email and any required funding or competing-interest information. Nothing in the package establishes that these declarations are negative or inapplicable.

Choose one PDF as the manuscript file rather than submitting both layouts as separate articles. Supply the main .tex together with the review wrapper if that wrapper is the selected compilation entry. The embedded bibliography makes the main .tex self-contained apart from standard TeX packages. The .bib database is supplied for editing and reference management.

Elsevier's LaTeX instructions state that files processed by Editorial Manager should be at one folder level. This ZIP therefore has no nested source directories. The README and these preparation notes are working documents, not manuscript sections.

## Generative-AI disclosure at submission

The supplied article itself has no AI-statement section, as requested for this working version. Elsevier's current policy nevertheless requires disclosure of substantive AI assistance with manuscript structure, organization or content. Assistance with research design or mathematical interpretation must also be described in the appropriate account of the research process. The exception for basic spelling, grammar and punctuation checks does not cover the substantive assistance used in preparing this manuscript.

At submission, the responsible authors must supply an accurate disclosure identifying the tool, its uses and the actual review they have performed. The declaration should not claim that author verification has occurred before it has. The policy places the manuscript-preparation declaration before the references; these separate preparation notes do not replace that required disclosure.

## Mathematical scope

The manuscript is a comparative exposition with proved intermediate results and special cases. It does not assert a solution of the general endpoint Ruelle problem, Bressan's mixing-cost conjecture, general BD transport renormalization, the primitive-periodic growth dichotomy, or the complete high-momentum classification. Proofs, quoted results and conjectures remain explicitly separated in the English version.
